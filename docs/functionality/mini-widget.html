Functionality / Mini Widget

Mini Widget

Ask quick questions and get responses without ever opening the full chat window.

What It Is

The Mini Widget is a small always-on-top input bar that sits in a corner of your screen. It's designed for quick interactions — type a question, get a short reply, and get back to work. It stays out of the way until you need it.

Opening the Widget

Enable the mini widget from the tray menu: right-click tray → Show Mini Widget. It appears in the corner of your screen configured in Settings → Appearance.

Tip The global hotkey (Ctrl+Shift+H by default) opens the full chat window. You can also configure a separate hotkey for the mini widget if you prefer.

Mini Response Popups

When you send a message from the mini widget, the reply appears as a small floating popup above it — no need to open a full window. The popup has a 10-second countdown before it closes automatically.

If you miss a response (e.g. the popup closes before you read it), it's saved and waiting for you in the mini history.

Mini History

Click the history button on the mini widget to open the Mini History window, which shows your last 50 mini widget responses. This lets you review anything you missed without opening the full chat.

Positioning

The mini widget anchors to a corner of your screen. You can choose the corner and fine-tune the offset in Settings → Appearance → Mini Widget:

OptionDescription
CornerBottom-right, bottom-left, top-right, or top-left
Horizontal offsetDistance from the screen edge (pixels)
Vertical offsetDistance from the screen edge (pixels)

Game Safe Mode

The mini widget is automatically hidden when Game Safe Mode is active to avoid triggering anti-cheat detection. It reappears when the game process exits.
